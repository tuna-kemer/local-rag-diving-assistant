1T7 BUOYANCY EQUIPMENT

Using the Buoyancy Compensator (BC): The buoyancy compensator (BC), also known as a buoyancy control device, allows comfortable swimming and resting at the surface and maintaining neutral buoyancy underwater. As divers ascend and descend, wet neoprene exposure suits change volume; the BC helps restore neutral buoyancy through these changes.

Controlled Ascent Practice: Ascents are performed by slowly venting the BC, not by inflating it. An uncontrolled ascent is one of the most dangerous causes of diving injuries. Ideal buoyancy for ascent can be summarized as: the ascent stops as soon as fin kicking stops.

Buoyancy Adjustments: As depth increases, ambient pressure increases, the air volume in the BC shrinks, and buoyancy decreases. The opposite happens on ascent. For this reason, the BC must be monitored continuously during descent and ascent.

BC Inflation Techniques: BCs are designed to be inflated both automatically (via a power inflator) and manually (orally). Oral inflation is performed while holding down the deflate button.

Points to Note: A BC should be neither too loose nor too tight. The weight system may be integrated into the BC; in that case, the diver must be well familiar with the release method.
