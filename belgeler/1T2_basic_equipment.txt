1T2 BASIC EQUIPMENT

The mask, fins, and snorkel used in diving are known as "basic equipment" or "ABC equipment."

Mask: The mask creates an air space between the water and the diver's eyes, providing clear underwater vision. Since masks enclose the nose, the diver can equalize the pressure inside the mask against increasing external pressure. Masks are made from silicone, plastic, and heat-treated glass. When selecting a mask, check the fit: place the mask on the face, inhale gently through the nose, and see whether it stays in place without the straps fastened.

Fins: Underwater movement is powered by the legs through fins. Fins come in open-heel and closed-heel (full-foot) types. Open-heel fins are used with full diving gear, while closed-heel fins are used for freediving and snorkeling at the surface. Open-heel fins are heavier and retain their stiffness under high pressure; closed-heel fins are lighter.

Snorkel: Allows continuous breathing at the surface without lifting the head out of the water. It should be 40-45 cm long and 2-3 cm in diameter. Snorkels with an exhaust valve allow exhaled air to be expelled by the shortest path.

Weight Systems: Since exposure suits and equipment increase buoyancy, starting a dive without a weight system is nearly impossible. Weight belts are standard 5 cm wide woven nylon or rubber. All weight systems must have a quick-release mechanism.
