1T11 SAFE DIVING RULES

Dive Leader and Role: The dive leader is appointed by the diving supervisor from among divers holding at least a three-star certification. The dive leader is responsible for pre-dive planning, controlling the group underwater, and overall direction of the dive.

Buddy System: Diving is never carried out alone. In the buddy system, entry/exit points, the route to follow, depth limits, and dive duration must be agreed upon in advance. A buddy check must be performed before the dive to confirm that all equipment is complete and correctly worn.

Briefing Requirements: The briefing should cover introductions, safety (bottom topography, currents, waves, emergency procedures), review (dive plan, duration, depth), equipment, discipline, and hand signals.

Phases of a Dive: Buddy check, water entry, surface movement, descent, underwater movement, ascent, surface check, surface movement, and exiting the water.

Buoyancy Check: With full gear and a full tank, enter the water, deflate the BC, hold a normal breath, and remain motionless. If the water level is at eye level, the correct amount of weight has been used.

International Dive Flags: The ALFA flag means "diver below, keep well clear" and has international validity; carrying it aboard a dive boat is a legal requirement.
