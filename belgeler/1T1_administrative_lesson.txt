1T1 ADMINISTRATIVE LESSON

TSSF/CMAS one-star diver training is organized under theoretical and practical instruction. Theoretical instruction consists of 12 lessons plus one additional lesson left to the instructor's discretion. Practical open-water sessions teach divers underwater communication signals, gearing up, water entry techniques, descent techniques, breathing, use of the buoyancy compensator, regulator use, mask clearing, basic first aid, and rescue skills. Practical underwater skills are taught through shallow-water sessions and a total of 5 open-water dives.

Admission Requirements: To join the one-star training program, candidates must know how to swim and be at least 14 years old. Candidates under 18 must provide a parental consent form. A health declaration form must be completed, and a doctor's report is required when necessary.

Medical Conditions That May Prevent Diving: Heart attack, heart conditions, uncontrolled high blood pressure, epilepsy, asthma and other lung conditions, ear and sinus diseases, pregnancy, diabetes, and panic disorder are examples of conditions that may prevent diving. The final decision is made by a specialist physician.

Certification System: The TSSF/CMAS basic training system consists of 6 certification levels: one-star, two-star, and three-star diver certificates, followed by one-star, two-star, and three-star diving instructor certificates. In addition, there are specialty certificates.

Certification Requirements - One-Star Diver: Maximum diving depth is 18 meters. Cannot perform night dives.

Certification Requirements - Two-Star Diver: Can dive up to 30 meters maximum, up to 42 meters when accompanied by an instructor, and can perform night dives.

Certification Requirements - Three-Star Diver: Can dive up to 30 meters maximum and can lead other divers underwater.
